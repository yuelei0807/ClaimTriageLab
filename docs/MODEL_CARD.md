# Model card

## Current status

No predictive or generative model is implemented in Task 00.

## Intended use

Future models may prioritize synthetic auto claims for human review. Their outputs are review recommendations, not findings of fraud or claim decisions.

## Prohibited use

No automatic denial, accusation, claimant contact, or investigation initiation. No use on real claims without separate data governance, independent validation, security review, and applicable regulatory review.
